VSL School turns the Vietnamese primary-school curriculum into sign-language
video lessons: 150 lessons covering Math and Literature for grades 1-5, based
on "App Khiem Thinh" (qipedc.moet.gov.vn).

FEATURES

- 150 lessons with full outlines - tap any section to jump straight to that
  part of the lesson.
- A video player built for learning: Vietnamese and English subtitles,
  playback speed control and fullscreen mode.
- Two subjects, five grades - filter instantly from the home screen.
- English support: English lesson titles, outlines and subtitles, shown in
  English or Mixed mode (English with Vietnamese underneath); Vietnamese mode
  stays purely Vietnamese.
- Offline capable: lessons stream from the CDN by default and can be
  downloaded in-app; the app checks for new content automatically.

DATA

Lesson content from "App Khiem Thinh" (qipedc.moet.gov.vn) - Vietnam Sign
Language Centre / Ministry of Education and Training. This is a personal-use
app, not the original work.
